About us

A Mogadishu travel partner you can count on

Active Travel & Logistics started with a simple promise: make travel to and from Somalia easier, safer and fairly priced. For more than eight years we have booked flights, guided visa applications and found the right place to stay for thousands of journeys.

Whether you are flying to Hargeisa for a meeting, taking the family to Nairobi or sending a delegation to Istanbul, our team handles the details so you can focus on why you are travelling.

  • Licensed, Mogadishu-based agency
  • Local and international routes
  • Somali, English and Arabic support
  • Clear prices, no hidden fees
