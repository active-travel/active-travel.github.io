# Active Travel & Logistics

**Your Journey, Our Commitment**

Active Travel & Logistics is a licensed travel agency in Mogadishu, Somalia. For more than eight years we have helped business travellers, NGOs, government delegations and families travel to and from Somalia, with fair prices and no hidden fees.

🌐 **Website:** https://active-travel.github.io/

## What we do
- **Flight booking:** domestic and international tickets at the best available fares, with changes and rebooking handled for you
- **Visa processing:** document checks, applications and embassy follow-up
- **Hotels & accommodation:** trusted hotels and guesthouses for business, family and group stays
- **And more:** cargo and logistics, airport transfers and group travel

We book local routes across Somalia (Mogadishu, Kismayo, Baydhabo, Dhusomareeb, Garowe, Hargeisa) and international trips to Kenya, Saudi Arabia (including Umrah), the UAE, Ethiopia, Uganda, Turkey and 100+ destinations in total. Support is available 24/7 in Somali, English and Arabic.
